About New Summit College (NSC)

NSC

New Summit College is a leading academic institution committed to excellence in education, innovation, and leadership. Since its establishment, NSC has focused on nurturing critical thinking, technical skills, and ethical values among students.

With strong university affiliations, experienced faculty, and student-driven initiatives like TechTrix, NSC continues to shape future-ready graduates equipped for global challenges.

2055 B.S.
Establishment of New Summit College at Old Baneshwor, Kathmandu, offering +2 level programs
2064 B.S.
Expanded operations with Tribhuvan University-affiliated bachelor’s programs
2075 B.S.
The first edition of TechTrix, a technology and innovation event organized by the NSC’s IT students
2080 B.S.
The third edition of TechTrix hosted at New Summit College, featuring a three-day hackathon
2081 B.S.
New Summit College held the grand opening of “Minds in Motion”, a pre-event for TechTrix Nepal aligned with World Environment Day
2082 B.S.
New Summit College signed a MoU with the Confucius Institute at TU to enhance Chinese language and cultural learning opportunities for students
