About New Summit College (NSC)

NSC

New Summit College (NSC) is a leading academic institution dedicated to excellence in education, innovation, and leadership. Since its establishment, NSC has emphasized the development of critical thinking, technical competence, and strong ethical values to prepare students for academic and professional success.

With strong university affiliations, experienced faculty, and dynamic student-driven initiatives such as TechTrix, NSC fosters a forward-thinking learning environment. The college continues to shape future-ready graduates equipped with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to meet global challenges.

Follow the timeline of events that mark NSC’s progress

2055 B.S.
Establishment of New Summit College at Old Baneshwor, Kathmandu, offering +2 level programs

The Vision, Mission, and Goals That Drive Us Forward

V - M - G

Our Vision

To establish and develop such an academic center where a blend of Science, IT, management and social science stands as bedrock in pursuit of nation/world building.

Our Mission

1) To build a legacy of excellence by providing theoretical, practical and research-based education.

2) To develop future doctors, engineers, politicians, scientists, managers, entrepreneurs, IT developers and technical experts through innovative, creative, value based and research based academic practices;

3) To initiate sense of social responsibility in the mind of new generation through the activities of corporate level social responsibility and

4) To produce skilful human resources in the area of productive and professional jobs.

Our Goals

1) To develop graduates in area of Science, Management and Law through optimum use of human and physical resources;

2) To provide globally competent education in disciplined environment with utmost personal caring;

3) To create conducive and research-based learning to groom out the inner talent of the students and

4) To maintain corporate relationships with different academic institutions and universities at home and abroad.
