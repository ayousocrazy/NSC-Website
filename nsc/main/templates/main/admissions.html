Admission Process

A simple admission process to begin your path as a New Summitian

STEP 1

Confirm Eligibility

{% if plus2 %}

Subject and Academic Requirements

Applicants must have successfully completed the SEE (Grade 10) or an equivalent qualification with the minimum GPA and subject requirements set by NEB and the college.

Please refer to the respective program pages for detailed subject and grade requirements:

Science Management Law
{% else %}

Subject and Academic Requirements

Applicants must have completed +2 / A-Level or an equivalent qualification with the required subjects and minimum grades as prescribed by Tribhuvan University.

Program-wise eligibility criteria are available on the respective pages:

BSc CSIT BCA BBM BBS
{% endif %}
STEP 2

Prepare Required Documents

After confirming your eligibility prepare these documents:

  1. SEE / SLC certificate
  2. Passport-size photos
  3. Citizenship / Birth certificate
    4. {% if not plus2 %}
  4. +2 / PCL mark sheet and transcript
  5. For foreign/A-Level students: Equivalence certificate recognized by TU
  6. Migration certificate
    7. {% endif %}
  7. Character certificate and other documents as required by the college
STEP 3 {% if plus2 %}

Get and Fill the Application Form

Where: Forms are available at New Summit College (Shantinagar, New Baneshwor)

Enter your personal info, SEE grades, choice of stream
Attach required documents
Submit by the given deadline (usually between Ashadh–Shrawan each year)

{% else %}

Entrance Exam Registration & Preparation

Where to register?

You can either register online from
IOST for BSc CSIT, FOHSS for BCA, FOM for BBM and BBS doesnot require an entrance

OR

Come to New Summit College, they will register for you.

Where to prepare?

We also have a platform called Learning Hammer. From here you can prepare.

{% endif %}
STEP 4 {% if plus2 %}

Sit for the College Entrance Test

Mandatory for all +2 streams at NSC

Tests generally cover English + Mathematics + General Knowledge + basic stream-related areas

Where to prepare?

We also have a platform called Learning Hammer. From here you can prepare.

{% else %}

Submit Admission Application

Get the New Summit College admission form

Submit the filled form with all documents — academic records, entrance pass result, ID, photos

{% endif %}
STEP 5

Entrance Test Results & Scholarships

{% if plus2 %}

Results are usually published shortly after the test

Scholarships is based on entrance test and merit list(rank order)

{% else %}

Results are usually published shortly after the test on the official TU website

Scholarship is based on entrance test (rank order) and plus2 GPA
